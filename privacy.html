01

Introduction

Welcome to Technical Presto. We believe that privacy is an important part of creating a positive, transparent, and trustworthy digital experience.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, and managed when users interact with the Technical Presto website and its conceptual music platform.

This document is intended to explain the privacy principles considered in the design of Technical Presto.

02

Information We Collect

The current portfolio website includes a contact form that may collect information voluntarily submitted by visitors. Other information may be processed by hosting or website infrastructure providers when the website is accessed.

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, selected plan, and message submitted through the contact form.
  • Technical Information: Information that may be processed by the hosting provider, such as IP address, browser type, and device information.

This portfolio concept does not currently provide user account registration, login, or music library functionality.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes related to providing and improving the platform, including:

  • Providing and maintaining website functionality.
  • Responding to questions, feedback, and user inquiries.
  • Improving website usability and overall performance.
  • Understanding general user interactions and preferences.
  • Maintaining the reliability and security of digital services.

Information should only be used for purposes that are relevant to the services being provided and explained to users.

04

Cloud Technology

Technical Presto is designed around a cloud-based platform concept. Cloud technology may support convenient access to digital features and content across different devices.

Depending on the actual implementation, cloud infrastructure may involve third-party providers that process or store information on behalf of a service.

Any operational version of Technical Presto should identify relevant providers and explain how information is handled through those services.

05

Data Protection

Protecting user information is an important consideration when designing digital services.

Appropriate technical and organizational security measures should be considered to help protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Security practices should be reviewed and improved as technologies and risks evolve.

06

Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a visitor's device to support certain website functions.

Depending on the technologies implemented, cookies or similar technologies may be used to:

  • Support essential website functionality.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Understand website performance.
  • Improve the overall browsing experience.

The current portfolio website does not intentionally install analytics or advertising cookies through its own scripts. However, third-party services used to host or deliver website content may process technical information according to their own policies.

07

Third-Party Services

Certain website features may rely on external services, technologies, or infrastructure providers.

These services may include website hosting, cloud infrastructure, analytics, communication tools, or other integrations.

This portfolio website uses Netlify for hosting and contact form processing when deployed. It also uses Google Fonts to load the website's typography. These providers may process certain technical information when visitors access the website or submit a form.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and information-handling practices. Users should review the relevant policies when interacting with external services.

Your Choices

Depending on the features available and applicable privacy requirements, users may have options regarding their personal information.

These options may include:

  • Requesting information about how their data is handled.
  • Requesting corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of information where applicable.
  • Managing available cookie and communication preferences.

For inquiries about information submitted through the contact form, visitors may contact us through the contact page. Requests will be handled according to the actual services and applicable requirements.

The availability of these options will depend on the actual services and applicable requirements.
